**Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis**
SQL · Retail Performance & Margin Analysis

Found that the Central region generated the second-highest sales but ranked last in profit, exposing a discount and cost structure problem hiding behind healthy revenue numbers.

---

**Nigerian Provisions Store — Vendor Sales Analysis**
Python (pandas, numpy, matplotlib) · Sales & Margin Analysis

Analysed 500 transactions across a full year for an Abuja general provisions store. Identified Beverages as the top revenue category at N1.47M, December as the peak trading month, and showed that 10% discounts cut profit margins by nearly half compared to full-price sales. Built entirely in Python with 6 charts covering category performance, monthly trends, day-of-week patterns, payment method breakdown, and discount impact.
